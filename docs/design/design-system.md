# On Now design system (exported from the design canvas, 2026-09-24)

Source: the "On Now" design system and the "On Now Home" design canvas in
Claude. This file is the text version, so the repo and Claude Code have the
same reference. Tokens are in `tokens.json` next to this file.

On Now is a stripped, cinematic iOS app that answers one question in two seconds: which of my series have a new episode today. It should feel like standing in front of a cinema poster: one show fills the screen, the image carries the experience, and the text only says what is new. Everything else steps back. The app is dark only.

## Colour

- Paint every screen `bg`. Put cards, rows and sheets on `surface`; the search field, season tabs and quiet round buttons on `surface-raised`. The search field has one look in Search and in My shows (its entry to Search): a pill 44 high, the tab bar's own magnifier at 18 in `ink-muted`, and its text at 17 regular, as iOS's own search field (owner, 2026-10-23).
- Use `bg-tint` only for large ambient areas, like the glow behind the Home hero. It is the hint of colour, not a surface for content.
- Set text in `ink`, meta lines in `ink-muted`, and upcoming episodes and data credits in `ink-subtle`. All three hold 4.5:1 on every ground above.
- Over a show image, make the primary action a white pill: `ink` fill with `bg` text. The photo already brings the colour, so nothing on top of it is coloured. Spend `accent` only on the primary action of plain screens without an image, and on small status marks (the follow check, the calendar day line, the hero's Premiere dot). The one other colour over a show image is `episode-finale`, the hero's Finale dot, beside its word. If a view seems to need two accent elements, one of them is not primary.
- Put `on-accent` on accent fills, never white.
- Fill a destructive action (Unfollow, revealed by a swipe) with `destructive`, with `ink` text. Use it nowhere else.
- Draw the focus ring as a solid 2px `accent` ring; it holds 3:1 on every surface.
- Use `hairline` for dividers only, never as the only edge of a control.
- Put `image-control-backdrop` behind a small control drawn on an image, so it reads on light and busy images: the date pill on the Home hero (with a background blur), the follow circle on a poster (no blur; once followed, `bg` at 35% over it, a little darker: owner, 2026-10-22), the pull-to-refresh spinner over the hero.
- Edge the date pill, the follow circle on a Home poster card, and the poster cards themselves with a hairline of `image-control-edge` (white at 16%, the thinnest line the screen draws), as Apple TV does. One token for all three. The circle keeps the same material followed or not; only its white icon changes, a plus to a check (a transition between them may come later).

## Type

- The typeface is the iOS system font (SF Pro). It is not bundled; the stack falls back to the platform sans.
- Trial: button labels (`button`, for example "Open in [service]") are set in Manrope ExtraBold at -3% letter spacing, bundled with the app. Only the `button` family points at Manrope, so rolling back is one change.
- On Home the show's logo replaces the title. Use `display` for the title only when a show has no logo. My shows and Calendar are named large and bold, as in Apple TV, but a quarter smaller than `display`: 26 bold, `display`'s letter spacing, left aligned at `header-inset` (16), as Home's logo, not the content's inset, `ink`, in a header 44 high under the status bar, Home's, so the title sits level with Home's logo, on `bg` (owner, 2026-10-23). `title` names a Show detail and the Calendar month; `headline` names cards and rows; a row (Shows, Search, Calendar) sets it smaller, 17 on 22, with lines about the show under it in `meta`, `ink-muted` (CRI-115): two in Shows, three in Search, beside its bigger poster. A Search row's poster is bigger, 78 × 120, and its follow circle is the poster cards' own, on the poster's top left, a little smaller (28) and 6 from the corner. The service line is the row's last, 4 more above it than the other lines. In Search, the status line comes right under the title, in `meta`, `ink`; year and genres are left to Show detail. The show's rating (TVmaze's, ADR 0018) sits on the poster's bottom right, 6 from the corner, a pill 20 high on `bg` at 80% (darker than the follow circle's) with its `image-control-edge` hairline: the number alone at 12 semibold, `ink`, no star.
- Set badges in `label`, uppercase: "TOMORROW", "FINALE". The Home hero's date is a pill in sentence case instead (see Home below).
- Write English, with original show titles. Never show a time of day. Use the data's own words, made readable: "Future uncertain", "Season 3 · TBA", "Season 4 premiere · Fri 9 Jul 2027". No em dashes.

## Shape and space

- Round cards and sheets with `radius-lg`, small posters and stills with `radius-sm`. Every control is a pill: `radius-pill`.
- Keep `content-inset` (24) as the screen's side margin for content, and `header-inset` (16) for Home's header bar and the screen titles of My shows and Calendar, which sit closer to the edge. Keep `space-4` card padding and padding inside controls, `space-6` between sections, `space-2` between inline items, and `space-10` around the Home hero.

## Imagery

- Home is image led: a landscape backdrop runs edge to edge at the top and fades into `bg` at the bottom. The badge, logo, meta line and button sit on the fade, never on the busy part of the picture.
- Pick the backdrop from TMDB by ADR 0012: the show's highest-rated backdrop, textless first, then highest vote average, vote count as the tie-break. No episode stills on the hero.
- Pick the logo from TMDB: the most voted English logo, PNG preferred. Always tint it to one colour from the tokens, whatever its original colours: `ink` on images, `on-accent` on an accent fill. Logos in colour make the screen too busy.
- Fit the logo in a box of at most 240 × 88, left aligned, so wide and stacked logos both work.
- No logo: set the title in `display`.
- No backdrop (CRI-100): the hero and Show detail use a poster instead, TVmaze's, else TMDB's, cropped from the top; a title in the poster may show, which is accepted as rare.
- A list's small poster is TVmaze's, else TMDB's (CRI-100).
- No image anywhere (CRI-100), such as a show announced but not out: no words. It never pulses, so it never reads as a skeleton.
  - The hero: a still, dark gradient from `surface` to `bg`, under the slide's own pill, title and button.
  - Show detail: no image area at all. The title opens the page, with as much space above it, below the floating header, as below it.
  - A list's small poster (60 × 90; 78 × 120 in Search): the same gradient, with the SF Symbol `photo.slash` (`photo` before iOS 27) in `ink-subtle` at half opacity, 22, and the poster cards' hairline edge (`image-control-edge`).

## Iconography

- Use SF Symbols, as iOS does. The one exception is the tab bar's four icons, drawn for the app so they share one stroke and can fill when active (see Tab bar below). The My shows icon holds the follow controls' own SF Symbol check.
- Services appear as TMDB's provider icons, clipped to a squircle with a thin dark ring (CRI-132, CRI-115): in the hero's "Open in" button; and in Shows and Search rows, before the service text, one per service in the text's order, up to four, 16 wide, 4 apart and 6 before the text, with the Premiere/Finale chip's light hairline (`episode-mark-edge`) instead of the dark ring, so a service the user has is seen at a glance. The text keeps the names, at `label`'s size (12) in a regular weight, `ink`, and is what screen readers read. "Unavailable" has a dot in the logos' place, 8 wide, in `service-unavailable` (a muted yellow, a warning).

## Home, direction B (chosen over A, the portrait poster)

Measured on a 390 × 844 screen, from the design canvas:

- Backdrop: full width, 580 tall from the top of the screen, cropped to fill (cover), centred.
- Fade: from 280 to 580, transparent `bg` to 75% `bg` at the middle to solid `bg` at 580.
- Content block on the fade: starts at 452, 24 side padding, 8 between items: badge (`label`, `ink-muted`), logo box (240 × 88), meta line (`meta`, `ink-muted`), then 16 down to the button.
- Button: full content width, 52 tall (a tad over the Apple TV reference, 47; 46 on short screens), a capsule exactly half its height round with Apple's continuous corners (iOS; the ends ease into the straight sides rather than meet them), `ink` fill, `bg` text in `button` (18): "Open in [service]". Page dots 16 below: 8 × 8, active `ink`, others `hairline`.
- "+": top right, 44 round, quiet: `bg` at 55% opacity, white plus icon.
- Tab bar: 83 tall, `surface` at 72% opacity with a background blur, `hairline` top edge; active tab `ink`, others `ink-subtle`. Replaced by the floating tab bar (see Tab bar below).

## Tab bar (CRI-133, Instagram style)

- A floating pill, fully rounded (`radius-pill`), centred, `tab-bar-bottom-gap` above the bottom of the screen. Content scrolls visibly behind it and ends above it (80 from the bottom). Home's hero is tied to the screen, not to the bar: it ends 183 above the bottom, as in the sketch.
- Material: `tab-bar-background` over a background blur (`tab-bar-blur`), edged with a `tab-bar-edge` hairline (`tab-bar-edge-width`). `tab-bar-padding` inside, `tab-bar-gap` between the tabs.
- Four tabs, icon only, no labels: Home, Calendar, My shows, Search. Their names are the accessibility labels. Every tab is the same size (`tab-bar-tab-width` × `tab-bar-tab-height`), active or not.
- Icons: soft, rounded, even-weight `ink` outlines, `tab-bar-icon-stroke` on a 24 viewBox with round caps and joins, drawn at `tab-bar-icon-size`. Home is a house with no roof overhang and a small door notch; Calendar a rounded square (corner 3) with two nubs and a header line; My shows its own box, corner 3 like Calendar's but 18 × 14.85, its bottom level with Calendar's, with the follow check and two short filled bars above it, narrower on top, evenly spaced: the gap between the two bars matches the gap from the lower one to the box's own visible edge; Search a magnifier with a large circle (radius 7.5) and a short handle.
- Active tab: a wide `tab-bar-active-pill` filling its slot, which slides from the old tab to the new one on `tab-bar-spring`. Home and My shows draw their icon filled in `ink` (the check dark on My shows' fill). Calendar fills only its body below the header line, flat at the top and rounded at the bottom, not the header band. Search is the exception: it stays an outline, with only the pill behind it, since a filled magnifier reads as a lollipop. No accent anywhere in the bar.
- Touch: one gesture over the whole bar, not a button per tab. Putting a finger down lifts the bar to `tab-bar-lift-scale`, the bar's only size change; it stays lifted until the finger lifts, as the sign that the drag is done, and never changes size on scroll. A tap commits the tab it lands on. A drag carries the pill along under the finger; nothing is chosen until the finger lifts, when the tab nearest that spot is committed and the pill springs into its slot, even if the finger has strayed above or below the bar. A tab change gives one selection haptic; landing on the active tab gives none.
- Tapping the active tab scrolls its view to the top (Home to the hero), or, on Search, goes back to the results and opens the keyboard. Search at rest has its title over the field, as My shows; typing, the title folds away and the field rises to 8 under the status bar over 300 ms (ease out, at once with Reduce Motion), narrowing for a round close button as tall as the field, 44, on the field's `surface-raised` with a white `xmark`, 8 from the field (owner, 2026-10-23).
