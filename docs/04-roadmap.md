# Roadmap

## 0. Discovery (now)
Vision, PRD, PoC scope and key decisions.
**Exit:** the documents in `docs/` are agreed and the open decisions needed
for the PoC are accepted.

## 1. Proof of Concept
See `02-poc.md`.
**Exit:** PoC success criteria met.

## 2. Design
Look and feel. References from Mobbin, design in Figma or Claude Design.
**Exit:** designs for Home, Calendar and Shows, plus a small design system.

## 3. MVP
See `03-mvp.md`.
**Exit:** MVP success criteria met.

## Release gate
Release waits until there is a budget for the Apple Developer Program
(99 USD per year) and the publisher (personal or company) is decided.
Until then everything runs on my own devices.

## 4. Beta and v1.0
TestFlight beta, then App Store release. Android through Google Play.

## 5. After MVP
In priority order:
1. iOS widget (FR-020)
2. Direct links to the show for more services (FR-021)
3. Explore feed with filters (FR-022)
4. Android widget (FR-023)

## 6. Growth (conditional)
Only if the app gets users. Costs and revenue are considered first at this
stage. Candidates: real push notifications, accounts and sync, a shared cache
for API requests, a commercial data licence, a paid tier.
